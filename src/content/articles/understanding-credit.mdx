Credit scores feel opaque, but the underlying formula rewards a small set of
consistent behaviors far more than any single clever move.

Payment history and credit utilization drive most of the score. Paying in
full and on time every cycle, and keeping utilization under roughly 30% of
your limit, matters more than the age of your accounts or how many cards you
carry. Closing old cards can actually hurt utilization math, so it's often
better to leave a rarely used card open with a zero balance.

A single missed payment does more damage than most people expect, and takes
longer to recover from than the anxiety around it usually suggests.
