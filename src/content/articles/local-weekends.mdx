A weekend trip two hours away resets more than people expect, mostly because
the planning overhead is so low that it actually happens instead of staying a
someday idea.

Pick a radius, not a destination, and keep a running shortlist of three or
four places within it. When a free weekend appears, the decision is which
one, not whether to go anywhere at all — most trips die in the "should we go
somewhere" stage, not the "where" stage.

Treat the drive or ride itself as part of the trip rather than overhead to
minimize; a two-hour trip with a good playlist is a different experience than
a two-hour trip you're resenting.
