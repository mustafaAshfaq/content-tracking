Local-first tools keep working, and keep your data, even without a network —
a property that used to be the default and now has to be chosen deliberately.

<Recommendations category="technology" articleId="article_technology_local_first_tools" />

The shape to look for: the app stores its primary copy of your data on your
device, syncs opportunistically when a network is available, and never
requires a round-trip to a server just to open a file you already have.
Sync becomes a convenience layer instead of a dependency.

This isn't an anti-cloud position — it's a preference for tools where the
network is an enhancement, not a single point of failure for reading your
own data.
