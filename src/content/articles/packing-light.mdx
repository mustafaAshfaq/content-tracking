A carry-on system only works if you can repeat it without thinking. The goal
isn't a minimalist packing list for its own sake — it's a routine that holds
up whether the trip is two nights or two weeks.

Start with a fixed set of "always" items: the exact toiletries kit, the same
packing cubes, the same charging pouch. Rotating clothing goes into two or
three cubes sized by category (tops, bottoms, layers) rather than by outfit,
which makes it trivial to scale the trip up or down.

The real trick is treating the carry-on as a container with a fixed volume
budget, not a list of things you'd like to bring. When something new goes in,
something else has to justify staying.
