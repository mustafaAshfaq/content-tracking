An emergency fund doesn't need a perfect target to be worth starting. It
needs a number you can hit in a reasonable time and a place to keep it that
you won't touch by accident.

A common, workable target is one month of essential expenses first, then
three, then six — treating each threshold as its own small win rather than
waiting to feel done. Keep it in a separate high-yield savings account, not
your checking account, so it's reachable but not one tap away.

Automate a fixed transfer on payday rather than "whatever's left" at the end
of the month; what's left at the end of the month is reliably zero.
