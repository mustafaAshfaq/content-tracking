The routines that survive a bad week aren't the strictest ones — they're the
ones with a minimum viable version that still counts as "did it."

Pick one anchor (a fixed wake time works better than a fixed bedtime) and
build everything else as optional layers on top: dim lights, no screens, a
short wind-down. On a genuinely bad night, doing just the anchor is still a
win, not a failure of the whole system.

Consistency in wake time, even across weekends, does more for sleep quality
over a month than an occasional "perfect" night ever will.
