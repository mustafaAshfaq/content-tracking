Most notification noise comes from defaults nobody chose, not from apps
people actually want to hear from constantly.

A ten-minute audit gets most of the benefit: open notification settings, and
for every app, ask whether a single daily digest would be enough. Anything
that isn't time-sensitive (most apps) goes to digest or silent; anything
genuinely time-sensitive (messages from people, security alerts) keeps
immediate delivery.

The remaining noise after that audit is usually a handful of apps — worth
revisiting individually rather than turning notifications off entirely.
