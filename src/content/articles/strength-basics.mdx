Strength training doesn't require an hour a day. A short, consistent
session built around a few compound lifts covers most of what a busy
schedule actually needs.

Squat, hinge, and push/pull cover the major movement patterns in one
twenty-minute session, three times a week. Track just one number per lift —
weight or reps — so progress is visible without a spreadsheet.

Recovery is part of the program, not a gap in it: two rest days between
sessions for the same muscle group is what makes the next session
productive instead of just tiring.
