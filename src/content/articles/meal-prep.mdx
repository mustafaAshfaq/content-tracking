Meal prep fails for people who get bored easily not because prepping is hard,
but because eating the same five meals on repeat becomes its own kind of
exhausting.

A base-and-variant system solves this: prep two or three base components
(a grain, a protein, a roasted vegetable) separately, then combine them with
a different sauce or spice profile each day. The prep time stays low because
the bases are the same, but the plate doesn't feel repetitive by day three.

Keep one wildcard slot in the week for takeout or a fresh-cooked meal — it
removes the all-or-nothing pressure that makes meal prep collapse after two
weeks.
